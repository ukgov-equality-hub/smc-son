About the Social Mobility Commission

The Social Mobility Commission is an independent statutory body that aims to promote social mobility in England. It carries out and publishes research – including the yearly State of the Nation report – and gives advice to ministers on how to improve social mobility in England.

Find out more about the SMC on the Social Mobility Commission website.
