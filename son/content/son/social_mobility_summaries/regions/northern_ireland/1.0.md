All areas are scored according to how favourable their environments are for the following 4 measures of social mobility: Promising Prospects, Conditions of Childhood, Labour Market Opportunities for Young People and Innovation and Growth. Each measure of social mobility combines data from multiple indicators into a single &lsquo;composite&rsquo; score. The scoring bands range from &lsquo;Bottom 5%&rsquo; to &lsquo;Top 5%&rsquo;, with a large majority of areas in the &lsquo;30%-70%&rsquo; band because most local authorities are very near the average. 

## Promising Prospects

This index measures how well young people from similar socio-economic backgrounds do in education and the labour market.

Young people brought up in Northern Ireland had the least promising prospects  placing in the bottom 5% of all local areas in the UK. This means they tended to have lower qualifications, earned less, and were in lower occupation levels by 25 to 44 years old compared with young people from similar socio-economic backgrounds across the UK.

Across Northern Ireland&rsquo;s results on the Index of Promising Prospects show that Northern Ireland is most falling below the national average and is in the Bottom 5% band.

## Conditions of Childhood

This index shows whether the conditions children grow up in are good for promoting social mobility.

Children growing up in Northern Ireland experienced the national average of conditions of childhood placing in the 30-70% band of all local areas in the UK. This means parents in this area have average levels of earnings, qualifications and professional roles.

[Read more on Conditions of childhood](/state-of-the-nation/drivers_of_social_mobility/composite_indices/conditions_of_childhood/latest)

## Labour Market Outcomes

This index shows which areas offer better or worse employment opportunities for young people.

Young people brought up in Northern Ireland had favourable employment opportunities placing in the top 15-30% of all local areas in the UK. This means they have more professional opportunities and higher earnings. 

Northern Ireland's results on the Index of Labour Market Outcomes shows that is falling above the national average for labour market opportunities.

[Read more on Labour market opportunities for young people](/state-of-the-nation/drivers_of_social_mobility/composite_indices/labour_market_opportunities_for_young_people/latest)

## Innovation and Growth

This index shows which areas help promote innovation and economic growth.

Northern Ireland fell into the national average for innovation and growth placing in the 30-70% band. 

Northern Ireland results on the Index of Innovation and Growth show that innovation and growth is falling around the national average.
